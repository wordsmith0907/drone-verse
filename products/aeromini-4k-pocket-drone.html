AeroMini 4K Pocket Drone (249g Ultralight, 3-Axis Gimbal, 38 Min Flight)

Ultra-compact folding 4K HDR drone weighing under 249g with 3-axis mechanical stabilization, 38 min endurance, and 10km range.

Engineered for Performance and Aerial Excellence

Engineered with precision aerodynamic principles and military-grade carbon composite reinforcements, the AeroMini 4K Pocket Drone (249g Ultralight, 3-Axis Gimbal, 38 Min Flight) represents the peak of aerial imaging and flight dynamics. Whether recording cinematic 4K/5.4K vistas, traversing complex indoor corridors, conducting high-accuracy land surveys, or competing in high-velocity freestyle runs, this platform delivers rock-solid flight stability, responsive control, and crystal-clear digital telemetry.

Key Engineering Highlights
  • High-efficiency brushless propulsion architecture optimized for low thermal dissipation.
  • Comprehensive sensor array ensuring centimeter-level positional accuracy and reliable failsafe return-to-home.
  • Digital HD low-latency video feed for seamless piloting and immersive viewing.
  • Rigid structural integrity tested against Level 5/6 wind shear and continuous vibration.

Smart Flight & Telemetry Integration

With intuitive ground station pairing, customizable flight modes, and automated return-to-home safeguards, pilots of every experience level can operate with total confidence. All firmware comes pre-calibrated and flight-tested by certified DroneVerse avionics technicians prior to packaging.

DGCA India Compliance & Operational Guidelines

Weighing under 249g, the AeroMini falls strictly under the DGCA Nano Drone category in India, exempting hobbyists from pilot licensing requirements under Drone Rules 2021. Features built-in GEO fencing and Digital Sky firmware compliance.

Pre-Flight Safety Checklist

  • Always inspect propeller lock mechanisms and carbon arms prior to arming the motors.
  • Calibrate compass and GPS locks in open outdoor spaces away from heavy ferromagnetic steel structures or high-voltage transformers.
  • Verify battery voltage cells are balanced and at nominal levels before takeoff.
  • Always adhere to the Ministry of Civil Aviation Green/Yellow/Red zone airspace regulations on the Digital Sky portal.
