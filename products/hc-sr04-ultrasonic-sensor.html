The HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance sensor provides non-contact distance measurement from 2cm to 400cm with ranging accuracy reaching up to 3mm. It includes an ultrasonic transmitter transducer, a receiver transducer, and an onboard electronic signal conditioning circuit.

Operating on standard 5V DC, the sensor can be interfaced directly with Arduino Uno, Mega, ESP32, STM32, and Raspberry Pi GPIOs. The principle of operation is simple: send a 10 microsecond TTL high pulse on the Trig pin; the sensor transmits an eight-cycle burst of ultrasound at 40kHz and raises the Echo pin high. The duration of the Echo pulse is directly proportional to the round-trip flight time of the sound wave.

Key Applications:

  • Obstacle avoidance robots and autonomous ground vehicles.
  • Liquid tank level measurement and smart bins.
  • Parking distance indicators and perimeter security alarms.
  • Interactive electronic installations and people counting.
