The Raspberry Pi 5 is the flagship computer from the Raspberry Pi Foundation, delivering a massive 2-3x speedup over Raspberry Pi 4. Featuring a 64-bit quad-core Arm Cortex-A76 processor clocked at 2.4GHz, alongside a VideoCore VII GPU supporting OpenGL ES 3.1 and Vulkan 1.2, the Pi 5 brings true desktop-class performance to makers, edge AI developers, and robotics researchers.

For the first time, the board integrates an onboard PCIe 2.0 x1 interface for blazing-fast NVMe M.2 SSD storage expansion (delivering over 450 MB/s sustained read/write speeds). Additionally, it introduces an onboard hardware power button, RTC battery connector, and dedicated UART debug port.

Major Improvements over Pi 4:

  • Broadcom BCM2712: 2.4GHz quad-core Arm Cortex-A76 with 512KB per-core L2 caches and 2MB shared L3 cache.
  • RP1 Southbridge Chip: Designed in-house by Raspberry Pi to handle high-speed I/O, delivering 2x USB bandwidth and dual 4-lane MIPI camera/display transceivers.
  • Dual 4Kp60 Micro-HDMI: Drive two simultaneous 4K 60Hz displays with High Dynamic Range (HDR) support.
  • High-Current Power Delivery: Supports 5V/5A USB-C PD power supplies, enabling 1.6A downstream current to peripherals.
