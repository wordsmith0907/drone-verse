Secure your DroneVerse account using standard TOTP authenticator apps.

Two-Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. In addition to your password, you will be required to enter a 6-digit verification code from your authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Apple Passwords / Keychain, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy) every time you sign in.
