# Comprehensive UI/UX Design System Specification: Zbotic.in

Based on thorough research and analysis of **Zbotic.in** (Moxie Supply Pvt. Ltd., Pune, running on WordPress/WooCommerce with a customized high-performance **XStore** e-commerce framework), here is the complete design specification required to rebuild the exact visual interface from scratch.

---

## 1. Complete Visual Design System (Colors & Tokens)

### Primary Brand Palette
*   **Deep Tech Navy (`--zb-navy`):** `#112D4E` — Main brand color used for the primary header buttons, "Shop by Category" menu, section titles, card borders/headings, primary buttons, and the main footer background.
*   **Deep Navy Dark (`--zb-navy-dark`):** `#0B1E34` — Sub-footer bottom bar, dark dropdown shadows, and spotlight gradient end.
*   **Navy Hover / Focus (`--zb-navy-light`):** `#1E3A5F` — Hover state for navy buttons, subtle active indicators.
*   **Zbotic Signature Coral / Orange (`--zb-coral`):** `#E95D2A` — The signature action/accent color. Used for CTA buttons, discount badges, active tabs, underline accents on section headers, search button hover, cart/wishlist counter badges, and link hover states.
*   **Coral Dark Hover (`--zb-coral-hover`):** `#D44E1D` — Active and hover states for primary CTA buttons.

### Secondary & Functional Accents
*   **Tech Cyan / Secondary Blue (`--zb-blue`):** `#007BC4` (or `#0D6EFD`) — Used for text links, product card category hover, search input focus outline, and technical tags.
*   **Success / In-Stock Green (`--zb-green`):** `#11A45D` (or `#10B981`) — Used for "In Stock" availability indicators, free shipping thresholds, successful "Added to Cart" button feedback, and eco/savings badges.
*   **Star Rating Gold (`--zb-gold`):** `#F59E0B` — 5-star product rating fill.
*   **Alert / Danger Red (`--zb-red`):** `#EF4444` — "Out of Stock" notices, clearance badges, form error states.
*   **WhatsApp Brand Green:** `#25D366` — Floating quick-support WhatsApp widget.

### Neutral & Background Palette
*   **Page Background (`--zb-bg-white`):** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white main content and card backgrounds.
*   **Soft Surface Background (`--zb-bg-light`):** `#F8F9FA` — Subtle contrast background for alternating sections, filter sidebars, and trust badge cards.
*   **Top Bar Background (`--zb-bg-topbar`):** `#F4F6F9` — Utility announcement strip background.
*   **Dark Footer Background:** `#112D4E` (Main footer) and `#0B1E34` (Bottom copyright strip).

### Typography Colors
*   **Headings & Brand Text (`--zb-text-dark`):** `#112D4E` (Primary Navy) / `#212529` (Deep Charcoal).
*   **Body Text (`--zb-text-body`):** `#495057` — Readable medium-dark slate for descriptions and paragraphs.
*   **Muted Text (`--zb-text-muted`):** `#7A828A` — Used for secondary meta, SKU identifiers, timestamps, and GST tax annotations.
*   **Subtle / Light Text (`--zb-text-light`):** `#9EA5AD` — Input placeholders, breadcrumbs, strikethrough MRP prices.
*   **Footer Text:** `#A0AEC0` (Secondary links) and `#FFFFFF` (Headings and highlighted contacts).

### Borders & Dividers
*   **Card & Section Borders (`--zb-border`):** `#E5E8EC` (1px solid).
*   **Subtle Dividers (`--zb-border-light`):** `#F0F2F5`.
*   **Input & Dark Borders (`--zb-border-dark`):** `#CBD3DB`.
*   **Accent Borders:** `#E95D2A` (2px solid underline for section headers and active tabs).

## 3. Layout Architecture

*   **Max Container Width:** `1440px` (Inner content `1320px`), margin `0 auto`, horizontal padding `16px` (`12px` mobile).
*   **Grid System:** Standard 12-column CSS grid / flexbox.

### Header Architecture (3 Distinct Rows)
1.  **Top Utility Bar (Height ~32px):**
    *   Left: Contact info & hotline (`📞 020 69134444 / 1800 209 0998`, Mon–Sat 9:30 AM – 6:00 PM).
    *   Center: Free Shipping threshold announcement (`⚡ Free Shipping on Orders Above ₹999 across India`).
    *   Right: Quick utility links (`Track Your Order`, `B2B Wholesale / Bulk Enquiries`, `GST Invoicing`).
2.  **Main Header Row (Height ~80px):**
    *   **Logo Area (Left):** Modern tech "Zbotic" logo (height 44px) + vertical coral separator line + 2-line tagline (`Electronics, Robotics & 3D Printing Hub`).
    *   **Search Engine (Center, max-width 650px):** Pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 9999px`) with `border: 2px solid #112D4E`. Contains category selector dropdown ("All Categories"), text input (`Search for products, drones, sensors, boards...`), and navy search button with magnifying glass SVG.
    *   **User Action Cluster (Right):**
        *   *Account:* Circular icon pill (`background: #F8F9FA`) + 2-line text ("Hello, Sign In" / "My Account").
        *   *Wishlist:* Circular icon pill with badge counter (top-right red circle with count).
        *   *Cart:* Circular icon pill with badge counter + 2-line text ("My Cart" / bold "₹0.00").
3.  **Main Navigation Bar (Sticky, Height ~50px):**
    *   **Left Anchor:** "Shop by Category" button (`width: 270px`, `background: #112D4E`, white bold text, hamburger icon on left, chevron on right).
    *   **Center Nav Links:** Horizontal menu: `HOME`, `SHOP ALL`, `DRONE PARTS & FPV`, `3D PRINTERS`, `DEVELOPMENT BOARDS`, `SENSORS & MODULES`, `DIY KITS`, `DEALS / OFFERS`. Badges attached to links: "SALE" (green), "HOT" (coral).
    *   **Right Anchor:** Phone Hotline / Support Pill (`Need Help? Call 1800 209 0998`).

### Hero Section & Value Propositions
*   **Hero Slider:** Full-width 1440px carousel with 5000ms autoplay, left/right circular arrow controls, and circular bottom dot indicators.
*   **Trust Pillars Bar (Directly below hero):** 4-column horizontal card strip:
    1.  *Free Shipping:* On prepaid orders above ₹999.
    2.  *100% Genuine:* Authentic robotics and electronics components.
    3.  *Same-Day Dispatch:* Shipped directly from Pune warehouse.
    4.  *Technical Support:* Dedicated engineer assistance Mon–Sat.

### Product Section Grids
*   Desktop: 4 columns (`grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)`) or 5 columns on wide monitors.
*   Laptop (992px – 1200px): 3 columns (`repeat(3, 1fr)`).
*   Tablet & Mobile (<768px): 2 columns (`repeat(2, 1fr)`) with compact 10px–12px padding.

## 5. Spacing & Rhythm

*   **Base spacing unit:** 4px / 8px grid.
*   **Section vertical padding:** `40px 0` (standard content sections), `50px 0` (major service showcases), `30px 0` (promo banners).
*   **Grid gap:** `20px` (desktop), `16px` (tablet), `12px` (mobile).
*   **Card internal padding:** Image container `16px`, body content `14px 16px 16px`.
*   **Section Header rhythm:** Title with `margin-bottom: 24px`, bottom divider line with `2px solid #F0F2F5`, and an accent `width: 80px; height: 2px; background: #E95D2A;` underline indicator.

## 6. Interactive Patterns

1.  **Card Lift & Image Zoom:** Hovering anywhere on a product card elevates the card by `-5px` with a rich navy shadow (`rgba(17, 45, 78, 0.12)`) while smoothly zooming the product image by `1.06x`.
2.  **Live Cart Counter Feedback:** Clicking "Add to Cart" provides instant optimistic UI feedback: the button turns emerald green (`#11A45D`), swaps text to `"Added ✓"`, and immediately updates both header and mobile floating badges. It reverts back after 2000ms.
3.  **Sticky Department Navigation:** The main nav bar sticks to `top: 0` when scrolling down past the header, maintaining access to the mega menu and category search.
4.  **Floating Quick Action Bar:** Wishlist, Quick View, and Compare circular buttons sit neatly stacked in the upper-right corner of the product image container.
5.  **Back-to-Top Button:** Appears smoothly at `window.scrollY > 350px` in the bottom-right corner with a hover transition from navy (`#112D4E`) to coral (`#E95D2A`).
6.  **WhatsApp Floating Concierge:** Fixed at `bottom: 24px; right: 24px;` in WhatsApp emerald green (`#25D366`), scaling up by `1.1x` on hover.

## 10. Distinctive Unique Characteristics of Zbotic.in

1.  **Dual GST Transparency Display:** Zbotic caters heavily to both hobbyist makers and institutional/corporate clients who claim Input Tax Credit (ITC). Product cards and checkout summaries explicitly break down `Base Price + GST %` alongside the total gross price.
2.  **Solaris Pill Search System:** A distinctive dark navy bordered pill search form that unifies category dropdown selection with a live instant search.
3.  **Comprehensive Engineering Documentation on Product Pages:**
    *   Tab 1: Detailed Overview & Pinout Schematics
    *   Tab 2: Technical Specifications table
    *   Tab 3: "Useful Links" containing direct links to GitHub repositories, datasheets, Arduino libraries, and circuit diagrams
    *   Tab 4: Verified Buyer Reviews
4.  **Authorized Brand Banners:** Prominently displays authorized dealer partnerships (e.g. *Bambu Lab*, *Creality*, *Waveshare*, *Raspberry Pi Approved Reseller*, *Adafruit*).
5.  **B2B / Volume Tier Callout:** Specific header and footer pathways for institutional purchases with volume discount tiers up to 40% and GST invoicing.
6.  **Mobile App Integration:** Direct promotion and footer badges for the "Zbotic.in Electronics Store" app on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
7.  **Service Spotlight Integration:** Dedicated banners for custom 3D printing services (FDM and SLA resin) and custom PCB fabrication alongside standard retail parts.
