# Comprehensive Robocraze (robocraze.com) Site Architecture, Layout & Design Documentation

This document provides the complete structural, visual, and architectural audit of **Robocraze** (https://robocraze.com/), captured for the purpose of rebuilding the site from scratch.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Design System Tokens

### 1.1 Color Scheme
Robocraze utilizes a vibrant tech-retail palette centered around energetic orange and slate dark neutrals:
* **Primary Brand Orange:** `#FF6B00` (Hover: `#EA580C`, Active: `#D9480F`) – Main CTA buttons, announcement bars, price highlights, active badges.
* **Dark Navy / Slate Charcoal:** `#0F172A` / `#1E293B` – Primary typography, headers, main navigation background accents, dark buttons.
* **Secondary Slate Muted:** `#475569` / `#64748B` – Secondary text, SKUs, breadcrumbs, specifications labels.
* **Background Light / Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` – Main page background.
* **Card & Section Surface Neutral:** `#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9` – Feature strips, sidebar filters, product spec tables.
* **Border & Line Color:** `#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1` – Card borders, dividers, input borders.
* **Success / Stock Green:** `#16A34A` / `#22C55E` – "In Stock" indicators, free shipping qualifiers.
* **Alert / Discount Red:** `#DC2626` / `#EF4444` – Sale badges, strikethrough savings tags, out-of-stock indicators.
* **Rating Gold:** `#F59E0B` – Star ratings for verified reviews.

### 1.2 Typography
* **Font Family Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif;
  ```
  (Heading accents frequently pair with clean sans-serif like *Montserrat* or *Inter*).
* **Font Sizing & Hierarchy:**
  * **H1 (Hero Titles, Page Titles):** `32px – 40px` (Weight: 700 / 800, line-height: 1.2)
  * **H2 (Section Headings):** `24px – 28px` (Weight: 700, line-height: 1.3)
  * **H3 (Product Titles, Block Headers):** `16px – 18px` (Weight: 600, line-height: 1.4)
  * **H4 / Subheadings:** `14px – 15px` (Weight: 600)
  * **Body / Paragraph:** `14px – 15px` (Weight: 400, line-height: 1.6)
  * **Badges, Tags, Metadata:** `11px – 12px` (Weight: 500 / 600, text-transform: uppercase, letter-spacing: 0.5px)
